# Kenney assets

The pictures and sounds in this folder are by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
free to use in any project, commercial or not, with no attribution required (credit is appreciated). `make-assets.ts` lays
them out as the files in `assets/`.

Each file is named after its pack and its name there:

- `tiny-dungeon-0084.png` is `Tiles/tile_0084.png` of the Tiny Dungeon pack, and likewise for Tiny Town, Tiny Battle,
  Tiny Farm, Pixel Platformer and Micro Roguelike (`Tiles/Colored/`).
- `tower-defense-0249.png` is `towerDefense_tile249.png` of the Tower Defense (top-down) pack, at 128 × 128.
- `interface-sounds-click-001.wav` is `click_001` of Interface Sounds, and likewise for Impact Sounds, Digital Audio,
  Sci-fi Sounds, RPG Audio and Music Jingles (`jingles_NES12`, the 8-bit set). The sounds are converted from Kenney's OGG
  files to 22.05 kHz mono 16-bit WAV.
